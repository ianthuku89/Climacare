What should I do during a flood*|During a flood, stay informed by listening to local news, and follow the advice of authorities. Move to higher ground and avoid flood-prone areas. Do not walk or drive through flooded areas as the water may be deeper and faster-moving than it appears.
How can I stay safe during a flood*|Seek higher ground immediately. Avoid walking or driving through flood waters, as even shallow water can sweep you off your feet or stall your vehicle. Stay away from downed power lines and electrical wires.
What supplies do I need for a flood emergency*|Have an emergency kit ready with essential supplies such as food, water, flashlight, batteries, first aid kit, medications, important documents, and a multi-tool. Include personal hygiene items and warm clothing.
What should I do in preparation for a drought*|In preparation for a drought, conserve water by fixing leaks, using water-saving appliances, and reducing usage. Store extra water and use drought-resistant plants in your garden. Plan for potential water rationing and stay informed about local water conditions.
Who should I call in case of disaster*|In case of a disaster, call emergency services such as the police (999), fire department, or ambulance service. Keep a list of emergency contacts including family, friends, and local shelters.

How can I prepare my home for a flood*|To prepare your home for a flood, elevate electrical appliances and utilities above potential flood levels. Install check valves in plumbing to prevent floodwater from backing up into your drains. Keep sandbags or flood barriers ready to use.
What are the long-term effects of a drought*|Long-term effects of a drought include water shortages, crop failures, increased wildfire risk, and economic losses in agriculture and related industries. Drought can also lead to soil degradation and reduced water quality.
How can I reduce water usage during a drought*|Reduce water usage by taking shorter showers, fixing leaks, using drought-tolerant plants, and collecting rainwater for irrigation. Avoid using water for non-essential activities like washing cars or watering lawns.
What are the signs of a drought*|Signs of a drought include lower-than-normal rainfall, reduced water levels in lakes and reservoirs, dry and cracked soil, and stressed vegetation. Prolonged drought can lead to water restrictions and economic impacts on farming.
How can I stay informed about weather conditions*|Stay informed about weather conditions by listening to local news, subscribing to weather alerts, and using weather apps. Follow social media accounts of local weather services and emergency management agencies.
What should I do if I get caught in a flood while driving*|If you get caught in a flood while driving, do not attempt to drive through the water. Turn around and find an alternate route. If water starts rising around your car, abandon the vehicle and move to higher ground if it's safe to do so.
What is a drought-resistant plant*|A drought-resistant plant is a plant that can survive with minimal water. Examples include succulents, cacti, lavender, and certain grasses. These plants have adapted to conserve water and thrive in arid conditions.
How does climate change affect floods and droughts*|Climate change affects floods and droughts by altering weather patterns. Increased temperatures can lead to more intense and frequent droughts, while altered precipitation patterns can result in more severe and unpredictable flooding.
What are some community resources during a disaster*|Community resources during a disaster can include local shelters, food banks, medical facilities, and emergency response teams. Check with your local government for a list of resources and services available in your area.
